# Earn: RHNVDA cash-secured put / covered-call wheel proposal

**Status: contract source implemented locally; not deployed or open for deposits. The Earn frontend is separate work.** The first strategy is an unlevered, weekly rotation between fully covered calls and fully cash-secured puts. Users deposit the Robinhood Chain **RHNVDA stock token** and receive `hNVDA` vault shares. An ordinary NVDA share in a brokerage account is not an on-chain deposit asset; acquiring or bridging RHNVDA is a separate user flow. The vault is separate from the protocol treasury Safe, with its own assets, share ledger and risk limits.

The legacy [CoveredCallDesk](../src/options/CoveredCallDesk.sol) is mirrored in this branch, but [its runbook](./COVERED_CALL_DESK.md) describes a separate treasury-to-market-maker RFQ pilot; its deployment status must be verified independently. [CashSecuredPutDesk](../src/options/CashSecuredPutDesk.sol) adds full USDG collateral and RHNVDA-for-USDG exercise for puts; it is source only. [EarnVault](../src/options/EarnVault.sol) implements user queues, hNVDA shares and a single call **or** put per epoch; [EarnVaultV3SwapAdapter](../src/options/EarnVaultV3SwapAdapter.sol) implements optional bounded USDG reinvestment. Neither the vault, adapter nor put desk is deployed. The existing [V2LiquidityVault](../src/v2/V2LiquidityVault.sol) owns a locked V4 liquidity position and cannot serve as the Earn vault. Deployment status and allowlists must be checked on chain before any live UI is enabled. Safe setup is a separate action after deployment. The [wheel mechanics and example put premiums](./CASH_SECURED_PUT_WHEEL.md) are documented separately.

The issuer describes Stock Tokens as tokenized debt securities giving economic exposure to the reference share without legal or beneficial ownership of that share, and states that offers/transfers have jurisdictional restrictions ([Robinhood Chain Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)). Earn copy and eligibility controls must reflect this. Issuing transferable `hNVDA` claims, lending against them and splitting income rights need their own target-jurisdiction review; wrapping RHNVDA does not remove underlying restrictions.

## User asset and share semantics

- The deposit asset is raw 18-decimal RHNVDA. Its `uiMultiplier()` changes the economic exposure of a raw token after corporate actions; the RHNVDA/USD feed already includes that multiplier. Do not apply it twice ([PriceOracle](../src/PriceOracle.sol)).
- `hNVDA` is a transferable claim on the **net assets** of the vault, not one NVDA share, one RHNVDA token, a fixed USD amount or a promised dividend. Its exchange rate can rise **or fall**. A primary deposit mints shares at the published epoch NAV per share; a redemption burns shares at that same epoch price, subject to rounding. Share count does not rebase. The initial seed permanently locks a tiny portion of shares to prevent near-zero share supply inflation attacks.
- A secondary-market `hNVDA` price may trade at a premium or discount to NAV because of liquidity, withdrawal queues and risk. Show `market price / NAV per share - 1` as a separate metric. Do not mint new shares at an arbitrary discount or premium: that transfers value between old and new holders unless a separately funded subsidy pays the difference.
- For the first release, retain free USDG for a cash-secured put **or** use the bounded adapter to buy RHNVDA for a covered call. USDG locked for a put cannot be reinvested. There is no simultaneous distribution of the same premium to users. Any future UI must distinguish option **premium collected**, realized vault P&L, `hNVDA` total return and cash distributed (initially zero).

## Weekly cycle and custody

1. **Queue deposits and withdrawals.** During an open call or put, incoming RHNVDA is held as pending stock outside the active-share NAV and cannot be offered as collateral. A withdrawal request escrows shares, but those shares retain economic exposure until the next priced epoch. Do not promise immediate redemption of stock or USDG locked in an open option.
2. **Close the previous option.** Use the appropriate desk's proved last Chainlink round at expiry. For a call: OTM returns RHNVDA; ITM normal `Physical` exercise has the market maker pay `size × strike` USDG and take RHNVDA; a lapse returns stock. For a put: OTM returns the full locked USDG; ITM physical exercise requires the market maker to deliver `size` RHNVDA before receiving the locked USDG; a lapse returns cash. The normal oracle path uses the fixed `expiry + 5 minutes + exerciseWindow` deadline, currently a two-hour window on each desk. Wait for the actual terminal state and receipt balances before pricing the next epoch ([call desk lifecycle](./COVERED_CALL_DESK.md#one-option), [put desk](../src/options/CashSecuredPutDesk.sol)).
3. **Reconcile and price.** Account for free and returned RHNVDA, returned or paid strike USDG, reinvested stock, fees, pending deposits, and any `owed` balance at **either** desk after a failed delivery. Price only with fresh, valid stock and USDG inputs and an explicit valuation policy. If settlement, transfers or oracle data are unresolved, defer the epoch and keep requests queued.
4. **Process withdrawals before new deposits.** Fix one pre-flow NAV per share from the settled portfolio; burn queued exit shares and reserve their pro-rata **RHNVDA plus USDG basket**, then mint shares against pending RHNVDA at that same price. The first seed has a 1:1 total-share basis, but a tiny fixed portion is irrevocably locked; subsequent mints use NAV. Each depositor's allocation is rounded individually. A deposit too small to receive even one share wei is reserved as a claimable RHNVDA refund. Basket redemption avoids forcing a potentially expensive stock buyback after physical exercise. Users may receive USDG as well as RHNVDA; the interface must display both before claims. If settlement or a fresh price is unavailable, hold the request in the queue rather than inventing a price or promising a Friday payout.
5. **Choose the next leg and roll.** With free RHNVDA, the Safe may request a covered-call RFQ for **no more than vault-owned stock after redemption reserves**, then use `offer`. With free USDG, the Safe may request a cash-secured put RFQ, then use `offerPut`, which locks the whole `ceil(size × strike / 1e18)` USDG amount at offer. Only one leg may be active per epoch; the allowed market maker `fill`s and premium arrives in USDG. After a call delivers stock and the vault receives strike USDG, those actual proceeds can back the next put; after a put delivers RHNVDA, that stock can back the next call. Reinvestment of **free**, unlocked USDG to RHNVDA through the guarded V3 adapter remains optional if the Safe wants stock exposure instead of a put. If an RFQ fails or is uneconomic, hold the existing assets rather than manufacturing yield. This first vault does not borrow to increase collateral or option size.

The vault, adapter and put desk are source-only and **not yet externally audited**. Operator/keeper procedures and launch controls remain to be completed. The desks' RFQ is a two-step, off-chain quote followed by on-chain `offer`/`fill`, without a cryptographically firm quote; an offer may remain unfilled and must then be cancelled ([call RFQ status](./COVERED_CALL_DESK.md#rfq-status-two-step-pilot)). The Safe must install the put desk **before the first deposit**; `setPutDesk` enforces an empty vault and can be called only once.

Actual Wintermute RFQs for RHNVDA calls **and** puts must be recorded separately from theoretical listed-NVDA option calculations. Token liquidity, issuer/custody exposure, oracle timing, strike, delta, exercise window and each desk's fallback can all affect what a market maker will quote; the two-year NVDA historical volatility is only a model input. Simultaneously selling a 400-token call and a 400-token put would require the 400 RHNVDA **plus** the put's full strike USDG collateral; the same ~$100k cannot fund both exposures at that size. The first release rotates one collateral pool between legs.

**Settlement caveat.** The normal ITM exercise path is physical in both directions. The legacy `CoveredCallDesk` can net settle even a `Physical` call through its owner-controlled 14-day backstop, so the vault must use the new [`PhysicalCallDesk`](../src/options/PhysicalCallDesk.sol). Its 14-day backstop can set a price, but an ITM result still requires the buyer to pay the full USDG strike before receiving RHNVDA within the three-day exercise window. The new **put** desk follows the same physical principle: the buyer must deliver RHNVDA to receive USDG, or the put lapses and cash returns. Neither path guarantees a Friday redemption or a specific asset mix ([physical call](../src/options/PhysicalCallDesk.sol), [put](../src/options/CashSecuredPutDesk.sol)).

## NAV and cash-flow accounting

At an epoch boundary, after the option is terminal and balances are reconciled, use a common USD numeraire:

```text
net assets = RHNVDA owned × fresh per-token RHNVDA/USD price
           + USDG owned at a conservative USDG/USD value
           − accrued fees − payable/redemption liabilities
NAV per hNVDA = net assets / active hNVDA shares
```

Count locked-but-still-owned **stock for a call or USDG for a put** exactly once while an option is open; exclude pending, unminted deposits. **Between epochs**, subtract the fair value of the outstanding short call **or put** from stock-plus-cash assets. Premium received on `fill` is cash accompanied by an option liability, not immediate investment profit. If no defensible live option valuation exists, restrict primary mint/redemption to settled epochs. The implemented vault does so: one option must terminate and both desks' owed balances clear before `closeEpoch`. Never value a USDG balance as risk-free cash without an explicit peg/liquidity policy. Do not double-count a desk `owed` claim and the corresponding token balance.

For one simplified cycle with starting stock `Q`, `q ≤ Q` stock locked, total USDG premium `C`, fill price `S₀`, strike `K`, and expiry spot `S₁`, immediate reinvestment buys `C/S₀` more RHNVDA before costs. If OTM, next stock is `Q + C/S₀`. If physically exercised, stock and cash **before** buying back are `Q − q + C/S₀` RHNVDA and `qK` USDG; buying back at `S₁` makes next stock `Q − q + C/S₀ + qK/S₁`. For `S₁ > K`, this may be **fewer** tokens than `Q` despite the premium. Slippage and fees lower both outcomes. The cash from exercise is return of sale proceeds, not a second yield stream.

For a separate put cycle, selling `q` cash-secured puts at strike `K` requires roughly `qK` USDG locked up front, rounded up to token base units. If exercised, the vault pays that cash and receives `q` RHNVDA; the put leg's expiry mark-to-market P&L is `C − q × max(K − S₁, 0)` before costs. A lower spot can overwhelm the premium, and the next covered call does not erase that loss. The put premium is not extra return on the **same week** of a fully deployed 400-token call unless the vault also had separate USDG for put collateral ([wheel calculation](./CASH_SECURED_PUT_WHEEL.md)).

Accrue management/performance fees, if any, against NAV under a published high-water-mark and fee schedule; credit protocol revenue only after earned fees exist. Publish per-epoch stock, cash, short-call/put state, collateral locked, NAV, share supply, shares minted/burned, realized P&L and fees, with on-chain reconciliation. User returns are `hNVDA` NAV total returns over matched dates; comparisons with an ETF must use its **total return** over the same period, not a distribution rate against this vault's modeled APY.

## Later products, each with a separate launch decision

1. **Cash distribution class.** Distribute a defined portion of *realized net* cash flow after assignment costs, fees and reserves. This reduces the distributing class's NAV. Option premium and a historical 44% NVDA volatility estimate are not a fixed monthly dividend or a forecast of achievable RFQ prices.
2. **Collateral and borrowing.** A lending venue could independently accept `hNVDA` shares, with a conservative collateral factor, fresh NAV/oracle checks, liquidation depth and pause rules. The RHNVDA locked for a call and USDG locked for a put cannot be pledged again. A borrow-to-buy-more-shares loop compounds stock, short-option, USDG, liquidation and funding risks; it is not part of the first vault and must have hard caps and adverse-path stress tests before any release.
3. **Risk tranches / tokenized cash flows.** Senior and junior claims require an explicit loss waterfall, reserves, priority of withdrawals, fee order and treatment of an unexercised option, put assignment losses or NetShare-backstop call. A separate income token must be backed by an assigned slice of actual vault cash flow; selling that slice reduces what remains for `hNVDA` holders. Do not count the same premium as both vault growth and a second product's distributable income.

### Leverage and protocol revenue: illustrative economics only

On $100,000 equity, a first 40% LTV borrow of $40,000 could buy and deposit more RHNVDA, leaving $140,000 gross vault exposure and $40,000 debt. Repeating the same borrow-and-deposit loop indefinitely has a *mathematical limit* of $166,667 exposure and $66,667 debt (`1 / (1 − 0.40) = 1.667×`). An actual lending market may permit less, and liquidation can stop the path early. With a **hypothetical fixed 8% borrowing APR**, no fees or liquidation, and an unlevered one-year vault total return `R`, the simplified equity return is `1.667 × R − 0.667 × 8%`. For `R = +45.8%`, that is about `+71.0%`; for `R = −4.4%`, about `−12.7%`. Both `R` values are conditional model replays, not vault performance, available RFQ quotes, a monthly payout or a forecast. The historical 44.06% NVDA volatility input is **not** an earned yield.

The product must show borrowed principal, variable borrow APR, collateral factor, liquidation threshold, health factor, available unwind liquidity and the possibility that call stock or put cash remains locked while a user's debt is liquidated. A 65% liquidation threshold applied to the idealized 40% starting debt/exposure ratio would reach health factor 1 after a 38.5% collateral-value drop, before accrued interest or fees. This is a scenario, not a proposed launch risk parameter ([Aave health-factor definition](https://aave.com/help/borrowing/liquidations)).

Protocol revenue has three distinct possible sources: a disclosed vault fee charged on *net* results under the agreed fee schedule; a lending origination/servicing fee; and a **real** interest spread only if independent lenders fund a lending pool and the protocol contractually retains part of the borrower interest. Borrowing against `hNVDA` does not by itself create protocol spread. Tokenizing a cash-flow tranche reallocates the same underlying P&L and may add a disclosed structuring fee; it does not create another copy of premium. Show investor return **after** all fees and financing costs, and show protocol revenue separately.

## Earn tab information architecture

| Surface | First-release content | Later, behind a separate risk decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vault overview | `RHNVDA → hNVDA`, live option/epoch state, NAV per share, share supply, token and USDG balances, actual 52-week NAV total return if enough history exists | Distinct distributing share class and its actual cash distribution history |
| Deposit / withdrawal | Wallet RHNVDA balance, exact pending amount, next pricing time, estimated shares at last NAV, fee/slippage, queued exit and claim status | USDG on-ramp and secondary-market hNVDA premium/discount |
| Strategy transparency | This week's call or put, RFQ strike, expiry, delta and actual fill premium; RHNVDA or USDG collateral locked; physical exercise and fallback status; realized P&L after delivery or assignment | Multiple strike-risk mandates or junior/senior claims |
| Borrow | Disabled with illustrative LTV, debt, borrow APR, health factor and downside examples clearly marked as simulations | A separately launched hNVDA-backed lending market with independently funded loans |

The planned performance chart should compare **matching-window total returns**, with `NVDA` buy-and-hold shown to identify stock beta, JEPQ shown as an options-income ETF benchmark, and every wheel, covered-call or leverage model marked as hypothetical until live vault NAV history exists. The $100,000 examples are normalized starting equity, not a claim of current vault TVL or 400 funded RHNVDA tokens. This frontend is separate work; no Earn page is shipped with these contracts.

## Release gates

- **Frontend as separate work:** label all RFQ premiums, APY and ETF comparison graphics as scenarios/backtests with dated inputs, fee and slippage assumptions, and a distinction between historical volatility and implied volatility. Keep deposit, mint, borrow and advertised distributions disabled until the matching contracts and product checks are live.
- **Vault before deposits:** verify the implemented epoch queues, NAV math, rounding, one-call-or-put state reconciliation, withdrawal reserves, guarded reinvestment and emergency pauses; test the full RHNVDA/USDG fork path, failed token deliveries and **both** 14-day backstops; obtain an external security review. `setPutDesk` must run while the vault is still empty, before the first deposit. Confirm RHNVDA issuer eligibility and token behavior before users transfer ([Robinhood Stock Token documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/)).
- **Operations before launch:** verify both desk listings/allowlists, market-maker RFQs and actual fills, fresh feed behavior, USDG and RHNVDA liquidity, Safe/keeper roles, monitoring, withdrawal capacity, disclosures and jurisdictional review. Issuer upgrade, blocklist, pause and `adminBurn` powers can freeze or impair vault assets; this is a product risk, not just an implementation edge case.
- **Leverage and tranches later:** require a live lending market willing to accept `hNVDA`, independently validated collateral pricing/liquidation, loss allocation, stress testing, audits and separate user-facing risk terms. A historical-volatility backtest alone does not meet any of these gates.
